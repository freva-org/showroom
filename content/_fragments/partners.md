Waterpark is hosted and operated by the Deutsches Klimarechenzentrum. The
datasets on it are produced together with research partners across Hamburg
and Bremerhaven.

**Get in touch.** Questions, a dataset you would like to see on the hub, or one
you are working hard and want kept on disk: write to
[waterpark@support.dkrz.de](mailto:waterpark@support.dkrz.de). For new
datasets and changes to the hub, a few times a year,
[subscribe to the newsletter](https://waterpark.dkrz.de/subscription/form).
